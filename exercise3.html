Taj Mahal

State: Uttar Pradesh
Best Time: October to March
A symbol of love and a UNESCO World Heritage Site, the Taj Mahal is one of the most iconic monuments in the world.

City Palace

State: Rajasthan
Best Time: November to February
Located in Jaipur, this palace complex showcases Rajasthani and Mughal architecture and culture.

Kerala Backwaters

State: Kerala
Best Time: September to March
A network of canals and lagoons, the backwaters offer a serene escape with houseboat experiences.

Ladakh

State: Jammu & Kashmir
Best Time: May to September
Known for its rugged terrain and Buddhist monasteries, Ladakh is perfect for adventure and nature lovers.

Golden Temple

State: Punjab
Best Time: November to March
A sacred Sikh shrine in Amritsar, famous for its golden architecture and spiritual peace.

Darjeeling

State: West Bengal
Best Time: March to May
Known for its tea gardens and panoramic views of Kanchenjunga.

Goa Beaches

State: Goa
Best Time: November to February
Popular for its vibrant beaches, nightlife, and Portuguese heritage.

Meenakshi Temple

State: Tamil Nadu
Best Time: October to March
A historic Hindu temple in Madurai, renowned for its colorful gopurams and architecture.

Mysore Palace

State: Karnataka
Best Time: October to February
A stunning royal residence, especially captivating during Dussehra celebrations.

Sundarbans

State: West Bengal
Best Time: September to March
The world’s largest mangrove forest and home to the Royal Bengal tiger.

Hampi

State: Karnataka
Best Time: November to February
A UNESCO site with ancient ruins from the Vijayanagara Empire.

Shimla

State: Himachal Pradesh
Best Time: March to June
A popular hill station known for colonial charm and cool climate.

Srinagar

State: Jammu & Kashmir
Best Time: April to October
Famous for Dal Lake, houseboats, and Mughal gardens.

Ajanta & Ellora Caves

State: Maharashtra
Best Time: October to March
Ancient rock-cut Buddhist, Hindu, and Jain temples and monasteries.

Ranthambore National Park

State: Rajasthan
Best Time: October to June
Famous for tiger safaris and scenic ruins inside the jungle.

Rishikesh

State: Uttarakhand
Best Time: February to May
Known as the Yoga Capital, with river rafting and spiritual retreats.

Mahabalipuram

State: Tamil Nadu
Best Time: November to February
A coastal town known for rock-cut temples and the Shore Temple.

Khajuraho Temples

State: Madhya Pradesh
Best Time: October to March
Famous for intricate erotic carvings and ancient temple art.

Konark Sun Temple

State: Odisha
Best Time: October to February
A 13th-century temple shaped like a chariot dedicated to the Sun God.

Valley of Flowers

State: Uttarakhand
Best Time: July to September
A UNESCO site famous for alpine flowers and stunning Himalayan views.
